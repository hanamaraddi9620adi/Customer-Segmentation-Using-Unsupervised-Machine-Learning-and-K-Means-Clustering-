From data to discovery

  1. Validate440 records. No missing values or exact duplicates.
  2. TransformLog(1 + spend), then standardize six spending features.
  3. CompareK-Means and Ward, with 2–8 clusters. Highest silhouette wins, with at least 22 customers per cluster.
  4. Stress-test20 subsamples and a raw-spending sensitivity comparison.

Good science includes the limitations.

These groups overlap. Removing the log transform changes the assignments substantially (ARI 0.094). Stability is not accuracy, and no revenue or retention improvement has been measured. Channel and Region were excluded from training.
